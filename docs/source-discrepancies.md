# Source discrepancies and limits

- The upstream 101 repository establishes a preconfigured trace, not learner
  construction. Its artifacts and completion evidence are not copied into the
  201 starter workspace.
- The factory environment has no approved OpenShift Virtualization cluster or
  managed Intel Xeon model endpoint, so all current presentation proof is
  REHEARSAL and no live model participation is claimed.
- Chart resources are requests and limits, not observed capacity. They require
  1-, 5-, and 25-seat measurement before certification.
- The local cleanup procedure is a lab objective, not independent Launchpad
  reclaim or zero-residue certification.
- GHCR release evidence, when produced, establishes source-bound artifacts and
  supply-chain verification only; destination approval and promotion remain
  Launchpad responsibilities.
